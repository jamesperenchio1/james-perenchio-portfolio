## Overview

A single-day hackathon build: a small Python machine-learning prototype aimed at optimizing
electricity trading by predicting demand and price movements. The goal was a working proof of
concept that could look at market signals and make a smarter call about when to buy or sell.

## The constraint was the point

This one is deliberately kept honest and short: it was **one day**, start to finish. That
framing matters, because a hackathon isn't about polish or production-readiness — it's about
scoping a real problem down to something you can actually stand up in hours, and getting a
working idea off the ground under pressure.

I keep it here because that skill — take an ambiguous, data-heavy problem, cut it to a
tractable core, and ship something that runs by end of day — is a real one, and this is a
clean example of it.

## Stack

Python and standard machine-learning tooling, used to prototype demand/price prediction for
electricity trading.
